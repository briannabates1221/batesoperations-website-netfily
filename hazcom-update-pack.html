What we need from you

  • Company name, address and the person who coordinates HazCom
  • About how many employees you have and the languages your crew speaks
  • The products you use: primers, adhesives, sealants, coatings, roof cement, solvents, fuels, aerosols, cleaners
  • Safety data sheets for those products. Phone photos, PDFs or links all work.
  • Where crews keep SDSs on a job (binder, truck, phone) and how you label containers they fill
  • Whether you work on sites with general contractors or other trades

We send a short intake form once you request your pack. Most owners finish it in 10 to 15 minutes.

What you do after delivery

  1. Read the program and correct anything that doesn't match how you work.
  2. Sign it and put a copy where your crew can get to it.
  3. Train your crew on the hazards of your products and have them sign the sheet.
  4. Update the chemical list when you add or drop a product.

We write the documents. We don't train your crew, inspect your jobsites or file anything with OSHA.

Who this is for

  • Roofing companies with roughly 5 to 49 employees
  • Owners and office managers who need a written HazCom program for a GC, an insurer, a prequalification platform or their own peace of mind
  • Contractors in states covered by federal OSHA

Who it isn't for, yet

  • Companies in states with their own private-sector OSHA plan, such as California, where rules and dates can differ. See OSHA's state plan list.
  • Respirator, silica, asbestos or fall protection plans. Those are separate programs. Ask us if you need them.
