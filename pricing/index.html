Possible areas to discuss

Bring one workflow that is absorbing too much time or control.

These are examples of operating problems that may come up in a conversation, not an established service catalog.

Vendor Turnaround Systems

Design a repeatable process for assigning, confirming, quality-checking, and escalating cleaning, maintenance, and turnover work across a growing portfolio.

Quality Assurance Workflows

Create consistent inspection, exception logging, rework, and sign-off steps so issues are caught before guests, owners, or team members feel the impact.

Damage and Maintenance Reporting

Standardize how damage, maintenance needs, photos, priorities, owners, and next steps are reported so issues move quickly from discovery to resolution.

SOPs and Task Ownership

Clarify the standard way work gets done, who owns each step, and when an issue should be handed off or escalated.

Lead and Follow-Up Tracking

Create a simple system for capturing inquiries, assigning next steps, tracking response status, and keeping opportunities from going quiet.
