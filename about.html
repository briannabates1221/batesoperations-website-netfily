About Bates Operations

Bates Operations is a small business in Killeen, Texas, owned by Brianna Bates. We write the paperwork side of safety for roofing contractors, starting with OSHA hazard communication programs.

Why we do this

Small roofing companies get asked for written safety programs by general contractors, insurers and prequalification platforms, often with a deadline attached. The owner is usually the one up on the roof. Free templates still leave hours of fill-in work, and full-service safety consultants can cost more than a small crew can justify.

We sit in between. You send us your information and your safety data sheets, and we hand back a finished, specific program you can review, sign and use.

How we work

  • Built from your information. Your products, your jobsite habits, your people.
  • Tied to the rule. Each section points to the paragraph of 29 CFR 1910.1200 it covers.
  • Plain English. Written for a crew lead to read, not a lawyer.
  • Yours to edit. You get a Word file, so you can keep it current.

What we don't do

  • We aren't OSHA and aren't affiliated with any government agency.
  • We don't give legal advice or inspect jobsites.
  • We don't deliver crew training or sign site-specific fall protection plans.
  • We don't promise any platform, insurer or client will accept a document.
